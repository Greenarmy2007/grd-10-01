Archival Vignettes

A Life in Moments

Grounded in the earthen discipline of the traditional wrestling akhada, forged through civic struggle, and remembered for selfless service.

1951 — 1975 • Formative Principles & Wrestling

The Discipline of Kushti

Shri Ghanshyam Dube’s foundational character was sculpted in the traditional clay wrestling arenas of Maharashtra. The akhada was not merely sport; it was a moral sanctuary cultivating physical fortitude, deep reverence for peers, and the duty to shield those who cannot defend themselves.

Strength is meaningless unless placed at the service of others.
1975 — 2024 • Public Commitment & Leadership

Quiet Civic Statesmanship

Through decades of civic advocacy in Mumbai and across Maharashtra, Shri Ghanshyamji was known as the leader who arrived before dawn and left after the last citizen was heard. He refused self-promotional plaques, believing that enduring work leaves its own quiet testimony.

The Pillar Principles

A Living Legacy

Four foundational tenets inherited from the life of Shri Ghanshyam Dube governing our selection framework.

Courage & Fortitude

Drawing from the rigour of traditional Kushti, standing firm for the vulnerable without calculation of personal gain or political safety.

Public Service

Dedicated grassroots intervention where civic duty precedes entitlement, addressing community needs without seeking publicity.

Unheralded Contribution

Spotlighting life work that exists outside mainstream television screens and social media algorithms, elevating quiet human substance.

Commitment to Society

Multi-generational impact on education, rural health, traditional athletic cultures, and ethical mentorship of future citizens.

Institutional Support & Philanthropic Leadership

Aryan Medical & Educational Trust

GRD10 is a philanthropic initiative of Aryan Medical & Educational Trust, dedicated to nurturing talent, recognising grassroots achievers and creating meaningful opportunities for future generations.

Philanthropic Trust

Aaryan Medical & Educational Trust

A non-profit philanthropic trust committed to public welfare, educational empowerment, community health awareness, and extensive charitable outreach programs across underprivileged communities.

  • Grassroots Health Camps: Free rural and tribal diagnostic missions conducting screenings for underserved communities.
  • Preventative Health Literacy: Community awareness campaigns promoting wellness and early health screening.
  • Educational & Social Welfare: Empowering marginalized youth through community development and welfare initiatives.
  • Supporting GRD10 National Awards: Dedicated institutional backing to honour quiet grassroots achievers and selfless social contributors.
Philanthropy & Mentorship

GRD10 Circle

Founded by Dr Sunita Dube, Radiologist. GRD10 Legacy Circle brings people together through philanthropy, mentorship and purposeful action to recognise grassroots achievers, nurture potential and create lasting social impact.

  • Grassroots Recognition: Honouring unsung contributors and champions of quiet service.
  • Youth & Talent Nurturing: Mentorship and educational pathways for rising innovators.
  • Collective Action: One Legacy. Collective Effort. Lasting Impact.
