Nomination Criteria & Evaluation Guidelines

The GRD10 Awards honour individuals whose dedication, achievements, professional excellence, and contributions have made a meaningful difference to society. Review the complete criteria and guidelines below for all award categories.

The GRD10 Awards honour individuals whose dedication, achievements, professional excellence, and contributions have made a meaningful difference to society. The awards recognise outstanding work across categories: Sports and Kushti, Education, Social Service (Uttar Bharatiya Ratna), Medicine and Healthcare, Media and Journalism, as well as Arts, Culture & Literature, Agriculture & Rural Development, Science, Tech & Innovation, Women Empowerment, and Public Administration & Youth Leadership.

Nominations will be evaluated based on the nominee's achievements, the impact of their work, their commitment to their field, and their contribution to the betterment of individuals and communities.

Award Category 01

1. Sports and Kushti

Excellence in Sports and Traditional Wrestling

This category recognises sportspersons, wrestlers, coaches, and other individuals who have made outstanding contributions to sports, particularly Kushti and traditional wrestling.

Evaluation Criteria

  1. 1
    Sports Achievements: Significant accomplishments at local, regional, state, national, or international levels.
  2. 2
    Contribution to Kushti and Sports: Efforts to promote, preserve, and develop traditional wrestling or other sporting disciplines.
  3. 3
    Training and Mentorship: Contributions to training athletes, nurturing young talent, and guiding aspiring sportspersons.
  4. 4
    Community Impact: Positive influence on youth participation, physical fitness, discipline, and sporting culture.
  5. 5
    Dedication and Perseverance: Demonstrated commitment, discipline, sportsmanship, and resilience throughout the nominee's sporting journey.
  6. 6
    Recognition and Excellence: Awards, titles, competitive achievements, and other relevant recognitions.
  7. 7
    Long-Term Contribution: Sustained efforts to strengthen sports and create opportunities for future generations.
Supporting Document Recommendations: Certificates of sporting merit, tournament scorecards, championship records, akhada or academy affiliation records, news clippings, and coach/mentor citations.
Award Category 02

2. Education

Excellence in Education and Educational Development

This category recognises educators, academic leaders, and individuals who have made significant contributions to education, learning, and the development of students.

Evaluation Criteria

  1. 1
    Contribution to Education: Significant work in teaching, educational leadership, academic development, or educational initiatives.
  2. 2
    Student Development: Demonstrable contributions to students' learning, personal growth, academic performance, or future opportunities.
  3. 3
    Innovation in Teaching: Introduction of creative teaching methods, educational programmes, or effective learning practices.
  4. 4
    Access to Education: Efforts to improve educational opportunities for disadvantaged, underserved, or economically weaker communities.
  5. 5
    Mentorship and Guidance: Commitment to guiding students, nurturing talent, and encouraging lifelong learning.
  6. 6
    Measurable Impact: Evidence of improvements in educational outcomes, student participation, or institutional development.
  7. 7
    Sustainable Contribution: Initiatives that strengthen educational opportunities and benefit future generations.
Supporting Document Recommendations: Academic institution accreditations, curriculum innovation briefs, student outcome metrics, testimonials, publication papers, or documentation of community learning centers.
Award Category 03

3. Uttar Bharatiya Ratna (Social Service)

Outstanding Contribution to Society and Community Welfare

This category recognises distinguished individuals and community leaders whose dedication to social welfare, humanitarian service, and community development has positively transformed lives.

Evaluation Criteria

  1. 1
    Social Contribution: Significant and sustained efforts to address social challenges and improve people's lives.
  2. 2
    Service to Vulnerable Communities: Contributions to the welfare and empowerment of disadvantaged, marginalised, or underserved individuals and communities.
  3. 3
    Community Development: Initiatives that encourage collective participation, social inclusion, self-reliance, and community welfare.
  4. 4
    Innovation in Social Service: Creative and effective approaches to addressing social problems and meeting community needs.
  5. 5
    Measurable Impact: Demonstrable benefits arising from the nominee's initiatives, including the number of people reached and the improvements achieved.
  6. 6
    Leadership and Collaboration: Ability to mobilise volunteers, build partnerships, and encourage collective action.
  7. 7
    Sustainability: Long-term commitment to social welfare and the potential for initiatives to continue, expand, or be replicated.
Supporting Document Recommendations: Project impact dossiers, beneficiary case studies, NGO/trust registration details, third-party audit reports, field photographic documentation, and community testimonials.
Award Category 04

4. Medicine and Healthcare

Excellence in Medical Practice and Healthcare Services

This category recognises medical professionals, healthcare practitioners, and individuals who have made outstanding contributions to healthcare delivery, medical practice, and public health.

Evaluation Criteria

  1. 1
    Professional Excellence: Demonstrated commitment to high standards of medical practice, healthcare delivery, and patient care.
  2. 2
    Service to Patients: Contributions to improving patient outcomes, access to treatment, and the quality of healthcare services.
  3. 3
    Service to Underserved Communities: Efforts to provide healthcare to economically disadvantaged populations and communities with limited access to medical facilities.
  4. 4
    Innovation and Research: Contributions to medical research, healthcare innovation, disease prevention, or improvements in treatment and care.
  5. 5
    Public Health Impact: Initiatives that promote health awareness, preventive care, sanitation, or community health.
  6. 6
    Ethics and Compassion: Commitment to professional integrity, responsible medical practice, and compassionate patient care.
  7. 7
    Long-Term Contribution: Sustained efforts to strengthen healthcare services, develop medical capabilities, and improve community health.
Supporting Document Recommendations: Medical council registrations, clinical outreach logs, free health camp records, healthcare innovation summaries, public health research publications, or institutional citations.
Award Category 05

5. Media and Journalism

Excellence in Journalism and Public Communication

This category recognises journalists, media professionals, and individuals whose work demonstrates professional excellence, responsible reporting, and a meaningful contribution to public awareness and society.

Evaluation Criteria

  1. 1
    Journalistic Excellence: Demonstrated quality, depth, accuracy, and professionalism in reporting, writing, broadcasting, or other forms of media work.
  2. 2
    Public Interest: Contributions to informing the public and highlighting issues of social, civic, cultural, or public importance.
  3. 3
    Integrity and Ethics: Commitment to fairness, factual accuracy, responsible reporting, and professional journalistic standards.
  4. 4
    Social Impact: The extent to which the nominee's work has raised awareness, encouraged constructive discussion, or contributed to addressing important issues.
  5. 5
    Investigative and Original Work: Significant contributions through original reporting, investigative journalism, documentation, or insightful analysis.
  6. 6
    Community Representation: Efforts to highlight underrepresented voices, local concerns, and issues affecting vulnerable communities.
  7. 7
    Contribution to Media: Sustained commitment to responsible journalism, public communication, and strengthening trust in media.
Supporting Document Recommendations: Copies of published investigative exposés, broadcast video links, editorial references, press club recognitions, and documentation of tangible public or policy impact.
Award Category 06

6. Arts, Culture and Literature

Excellence in Arts, Culture and Literary Heritage

This category recognises artists, writers, poets, cultural custodians, and performers whose dedicated creative work preserves cultural heritage, promotes traditional and contemporary arts, and enriches society.

Evaluation Criteria

  1. 1
    Cultural Contribution: Significant and enduring contributions to visual arts, performing arts, folklore, literature, or classical traditions.
  2. 2
    Preservation of Heritage: Sustained efforts to preserve, document, or revive traditional art forms, crafts, regional languages, or oral histories.
  3. 3
    Training and Mentorship: Dedication to artistic mentorship, training younger generations, and nurturing emerging artistic talent.
  4. 4
    Community and Social Resonance: Creative works that address important social themes, foster cultural harmony, and inspire ethical reflection.
  5. 5
    Artistic Excellence & Mastery: Demonstrated excellence, originality, craftsmanship, and adherence to high artistic and aesthetic standards.
  6. 6
    Cultural Access & Representation: Efforts to make cultural heritage and literary arts accessible to diverse and underserved communities.
  7. 7
    Sustained Creative Commitment: Lifelong dedication, perseverance, and sustained contribution to the enrichment of national culture.
Supporting Document Recommendations: Published literary works, exhibition catalogues, performance archives, cultural trust accreditations, press reviews, and documentation of heritage conservation.
Award Category 07

7. Agriculture and Rural Development

Excellence in Sustainable Agriculture and Rural Transformation

This category recognises agrarian pioneers, grassroots farmers, water conservationists, and rural reformers whose innovative efforts advance sustainable farming, food security, and village empowerment.

Evaluation Criteria

  1. 1
    Sustainable Agricultural Practices: Adoption and promotion of eco-friendly farming, organic cultivation, indigenous seed conservation, or soil health management.
  2. 2
    Water & Natural Resource Conservation: Groundbreaking initiatives in rainwater harvesting, watershed management, micro-irrigation, or drought resilience.
  3. 3
    Rural Livelihoods & Farmer Income: Tangible contributions to increasing farm incomes, reducing cultivation costs, and enhancing rural livelihood resilience.
  4. 4
    Grassroots Collectivisation: Formation and leadership of farmer-producer organizations, cooperatives, or self-help federations that empower smallholders.
  5. 5
    Agricultural Innovation & Technology: Frugal farming tools, post-harvest processing innovations, or climate-resilient crop practices tailored for village reality.
  6. 6
    Community Empowerment & Youth Engagement: Motivating rural youth to embrace modern sustainable farming, curbing distress migration, and fostering village self-reliance.
  7. 7
    Long-Term Rural Contribution: Sustained dedication to agrarian welfare, rural dignity, and strengthening India's rural economy.
Supporting Document Recommendations: Farm yield records, watershed project documentation, village panchayat citations, FPO/cooperative audit certificates, agricultural university commendations, and field photographs.
Award Category 08

8. Science, Technology and Innovation

Excellence in Science, Technology and Practical Innovation

This category recognises grassroots scientists, inventors, engineers, and technologists whose frugal innovations and scientific research solve real-world problems for underserved communities.

Evaluation Criteria

  1. 1
    Scientific & Technological Excellence: High standards of scientific inquiry, engineering rigor, and technical problem-solving.
  2. 2
    Grassroots & Frugal Innovation: Creation of low-cost, scalable, and practical innovations addressing urgent civic, rural, or healthcare challenges.
  3. 3
    Societal & Environmental Impact: Demonstrated improvements in living standards, clean energy, waste management, sanitation, or digital accessibility.
  4. 4
    Scientific Temper & Education: Promoting rational thinking, scientific temper, and tech literacy among students, especially in underprivileged schools.
  5. 5
    Ethics and Responsible Tech: Commitment to open-source access, sustainability, user safety, and human-centric engineering.
  6. 6
    Collaborative Capability: Building interdisciplinary partnerships, open technical resources, and empowering community-level problem-solvers.
  7. 7
    Sustained Research & Innovation: Continuous commitment to technical innovation, research dissemination, and nation-building applications.
Supporting Document Recommendations: Patent or open-source documentation, field trial reports, technical whitepapers, community impact audits, institutional endorsements, and demonstration videos.
Award Category 09

9. Women Empowerment

Excellence in Gender Equity, Female Leadership and Grassroots Upliftment

This category recognises pioneering women, community organizers, and institutions whose courageous work advances gender equity, economic self-reliance, female safety, education, and grassroots leadership.

Evaluation Criteria

  1. 1
    Grassroots Women's Collectivisation: Organising women into self-help groups, cooperative societies, and micro-enterprises for economic autonomy.
  2. 2
    Educational & Vocational Access: Significant work in girl-child education, digital literacy, vocational training, and career readiness.
  3. 3
    Safety, Legal Rights & Health Advocacy: Courageous defense of women's safety, legal rights, reproductive health literacy, and protection against discrimination.
  4. 4
    Female Leadership Development: Mentoring women to assume active decision-making roles in community councils, panchayats, and civic life.
  5. 5
    Measurable Transformation: Demonstrable improvements in the standard of living, financial security, and dignity of marginalized women.
  6. 6
    Resilience and Role Modeling: Overcoming severe socio-economic adversity to build inspiring pathways for others.
  7. 7
    Enduring Institutional Impact: Building sustainable organizations, shelters, or networks that will safeguard and uplift women for generations.
Supporting Document Recommendations: SHG federation reports, vocational training outcome data, legal advocacy case logs, beneficiary testimonials, independent audit reports, and media documentation.
Award Category 10

10. Public Administration and Youth Leadership

Excellence in Civic Governance, Ethical Public Service and Youth Mentorship

This category recognises exemplary civic administrators, grassroots youth mobilisers, and public servants who exemplify unbending integrity, civic stewardship, and youth empowerment.

Evaluation Criteria

  1. 1
    Ethical Governance & Transparency: Demonstrated commitment to honesty, accountability, and citizen-first delivery in public duties.
  2. 2
    Last-Mile Public Service Delivery: Reforms or initiatives that eliminate bureaucratic hurdles and deliver critical entitlements to underprivileged citizens.
  3. 3
    Youth Mobilisation & Nation Building: Inspiring, organising, and mentoring youth for constructive community service, disaster response, civic hygiene, and sports.
  4. 4
    Innovation in Civic Administration: Adoption of citizen-friendly administrative processes, digital grievance redressal, or participatory governance.
  5. 5
    Crisis Leadership & Community Harmony: Exemplary resilience, empathy, and decisive leadership during public emergencies or social challenges.
  6. 6
    Mentorship & Character Building: Guiding at-risk youth toward skilled livelihoods, civic responsibility, and positive societal participation.
  7. 7
    Long-Term Institutional Legacy: Sustained contributions that rebuild public trust, strengthen democratic institutions, and inspire the next generation of civil servants.
Supporting Document Recommendations: Public administration audit reports, citizen charter implementation briefs, youth program records, community testimonials, government commendations, and documentation of verified administrative reforms.

The following institutional principles apply across all award categories to evaluate nominations:

1. Evidence of Achievement
Nominations should provide factual information and supporting evidence of the nominee's work wherever available.
2. Demonstrable Impact
Preference should be given to contributions with clear, credible, and meaningful outcomes.
3. Commitment and Leadership
The nominee's dedication, integrity, initiative, and influence within their field will be considered.
4. Sustained Contribution
The duration, consistency, and long-term significance of the nominee's work will be taken into account.
5. Supporting Documentation
Relevant certificates, awards, publications, photographs, testimonials, records, or other supporting documents may be submitted where applicable.
6. Verification Protocol
The GRD10 Awards Committee may verify the information provided and request additional documentation when necessary.

Selection Principle: The final selection will be based on the overall merits of each nomination, its alignment with the relevant award category, and the nominee's contribution to society.

Note: Submission of a nomination does not guarantee selection. The GRD10 Awards Committee reserves the right to evaluate nominations and make the final decision regarding the award recipients.

Institutional Privacy & Nomination Data Policy

Collection & Purpose: All candidate details, personal contact information, testimonials, and supporting documents submitted via this portal are collected exclusively to facilitate independent candidate assessment for the GRD10 National Awards.

Restricted Access: Access to nomination dossiers and uploaded files is strictly restricted to members of the Honorary Selection Committee convened by Aryan Medical & Educational Trust. Candidate records are never sold, commercialized, or shared with third parties.

Data Retention & Security: Unselected nomination files are securely retired and purged following the conclusion of each annual convocation cycle. Selected awardee biographies are archived in the permanent institutional registry with prior consent.
