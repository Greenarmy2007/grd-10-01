Award Category 01

Sports and Kushti

Synchronized with Nomination Form below

Recognising sportspersons, wrestlers, coaches, and individuals who have made outstanding contributions to sports, particularly Kushti and traditional wrestling.

Specific Evaluation Criteria

Supporting Document Recommendations: Certificates, championship rankings, tournament scorecards, akhada or academy affiliation records, news clippings, and coach/mentor testimonials.
Ready to file? This category is pre-selected in the submission dossier. Proceed to Nomination Form ↓

In addition to category-specific considerations, all nominations submitted to the GRD10 National Awards Selection Committee are adjudicated under uniform institutional standards:

1. Evidence of Achievement
Applicants should provide factual information and relevant supporting evidence of the nominee's work.
2. Demonstrable Impact
Consider the credibility, significance, and concrete outcomes of the nominee's contributions.
3. Commitment & Leadership
Consider dedication, integrity, initiative, and positive influence within the relevant field.
4. Sustained Contribution
Consider the duration, consistency, and long-term significance of the nominee's service.
5. Supporting Documentation
Applicants may provide relevant certificates, awards, publications, photographs, testimonials, records, and other supporting documents, as applicable.
6. Verification Protocol
GRD10 may verify submitted information or request additional documentation from nominators or institutions.
Notice: Submission of a nomination does not guarantee selection. The GRD10 Awards Committee will evaluate nominations based on their overall merits, alignment with the relevant award category, and contribution to society.

Institutional Privacy & Nomination Data Policy

Collection & Purpose: All candidate details, personal contact information, testimonials, and supporting documents submitted via this portal are collected exclusively to facilitate independent candidate assessment for the GRD10 National Awards.

Restricted Access: Access to nomination dossiers and uploaded files is strictly restricted to members of the Honorary Selection Committee convened by Aryan Medical & Educational Trust. Candidate records are never sold, commercialized, or shared with third parties.

Data Retention & Security: Unselected nomination files are securely retired and purged following the conclusion of each annual convocation cycle. Selected awardee biographies are archived in the permanent institutional registry with prior consent.
