Selection Framework

The 4-Stage Selection & Evaluation Process

Every nomination is scrutinized under strict institutional guidelines prioritizing verifiable ground impact over commercial visibility.

Stage 01

Public Call

Nationwide nominations invited across sport, healthcare, education, Uttar Bharatiya Ratna, and public dedication.

Stage 02

Dossier Review

Secretariat conducts preliminary verification of credentials, testimonials, media records, and references.

Stage 03

Committee Review

The Honorary Selection Committee deliberates and selects the 10 quiet contributors who exemplify the legacy.

Stage 04

Annual Convocation

Official conferment ceremony in Mumbai on 10 November, celebrating 10 Achievers — 1 Lasting Legacy.

Selection Governance

How Achievers Are Selected

1. Public & Field Nominations

Nominations are accepted openly through our secure portal from citizens, field volunteers, regional journalists, and civic institutions.

2. Independent Ground Fact-Checking

A dedicated research team verifies the nominee's life work, visiting communities and reviewing documentation to confirm genuine societal impact and absence of commercial marketing.

3. Selection Committee Ratification

The honorary panel evaluates candidates solely against the virtues of courage, public service, integrity, and perseverance.
