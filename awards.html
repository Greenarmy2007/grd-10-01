Selection Governance

How Achievers Are Selected

1. Public & Field Nominations

Nominations are accepted openly through our secure portal from citizens, field volunteers, regional journalists, and civic institutions.

2. Independent Ground Fact-Checking

A dedicated research team verifies the nominee's life work, visiting communities and reviewing documentation to confirm genuine societal impact and absence of commercial marketing.

3. Selection Committee Ratification

The honorary panel evaluates candidates solely against the virtues of courage, public service, integrity, and perseverance.
